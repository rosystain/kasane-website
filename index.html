为本地与自建漫画库打造的沉浸式阅读器

iOS · iPadOS · macOS · Apple Vision Pro

Download on theApp Store 了解更多

一个 App，整个漫画库

Kasane 把散落各处的漫画收进同一个书架，并以原生体验呈现。

多来源，一个书架

支持本地 CBZ/ZIP、PDF 与 EPUB（纯图片漫画）文件，以及 OPDS 1.2 / 2.0 自建服务器（Komga、Kavita、Stump 等）。

沉浸式阅读

分页、条漫连续滚动与双页拼页，支持 RTL 漫画；翻页方向、点按区域、背景色均可自定义，每部作品独立记忆。

离线与缓存

一键保存章节离线阅读；已读章节自动归档到持久缓存，智能容量管理随时可查、可清理。

书架与进度

系列聚合、阅读进度、全局与库内搜索一应俱全；连接 OPDS 服务器时阅读进度可双向同步。

隐私优先

不收集、不上传、不追踪任何数据。你的漫画与阅读记录只保存在你的设备上，只属于你。

为 Apple 平台而生

以原生 SwiftUI 构建，iPhone、iPad、Mac 与 Apple Vision Pro 上均有一致而流畅的体验。

你的数据，只在你手里

Kasane 不含任何统计或追踪组件。服务器地址与凭据保存在你设备的钥匙串中，只会发送给你自己指定的服务器。详见隐私政策。

开启 iCloud 钥匙串时，凭据会经 Apple 端到端加密在你的设备间同步。

An immersive manga reader for your local and self-hosted library

iOS · iPadOS · macOS · Apple Vision Pro

Download on theApp Store Learn more

One app, your whole manga library

Kasane brings manga from every source into a single shelf, presented with a truly native experience.

Every source, one shelf

Read local CBZ/ZIP, PDF and EPUB (picture-book comics) files, or browse OPDS 1.2 / 2.0 self-hosted servers such as Komga, Kavita and Stump.

Immersive reading

Paged, continuous webtoon and dual-page modes with RTL support. Reading direction, tap zones and background are all customizable — remembered per series.

Offline & cache

Save chapters for offline reading with one tap. Finished chapters are archived automatically, with smart, transparent cache management.

Shelf & progress

Series aggregation, reading progress and powerful search throughout. With OPDS servers, progress syncs in both directions.

Privacy first

No collection, no upload, no tracking. Your manga and reading history stay on your device — where they belong.

Built for Apple platforms

Native SwiftUI throughout — a consistent, fluid experience on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro.

Your data stays yours

Kasane contains no analytics or tracking components of any kind. Server addresses and credentials live in your device's Keychain and are sent only to the server you specify. See the privacy policy for details.

If iCloud Keychain is enabled, credentials sync across your own devices with Apple's end-to-end encryption.
